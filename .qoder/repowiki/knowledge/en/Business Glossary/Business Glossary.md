### HomeCare
- Definition：The project's product name: a mobile-first digital healthcare application for residential medical consultations in Rwanda (default location Kigali, prices in RWF), supporting patient doctor search/booking/visit tracking and doctor visit execution with clinical notes, electronic prescriptions, and lab results.
- Aliases：homecare

### RMDC license
- Definition：Rwanda Medical & Dental Council license number used as the professional credential identifier for doctors; displayed in doctor profiles and managed through the admin portal's doctor verification flow (approve/suspend).
- Aliases：RMDC number、licenseNumber

### coverage districts
- Definition：Kigali geographic zones a doctor is authorized to serve; collected during doctor registration and used as a filter when patients search for specialists.

### SAMU 112
- Definition：Rwanda's national emergency service helpline referenced by the EmergencyBanner component as the emergency disclaimer shown to patients.

### Rx
- Definition：Electronic prescriptions published by doctors during a consultation; stored alongside clinical summaries and lab results in the patient's chronological medical history.
- Aliases：electronic prescriptions
